## Problem

One quality video a day, by hand, doesn't scale. The bottleneck isn't ideas — it's production and publishing, every single day.

## Approach

Treat the channel as a system, not a habit:

- The current 79-video series (JLPT N5 stroke order) was **generated, not recorded** — stroke order rendered programmatically from KanjiVG open data at 1080×1920, with covers and captions produced in one batch run.
- Publishing is scheduled daily at 23:00 JST to **both** YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, running through February 5, 2027 with no daily manual work.
- Incidents are handled deliberately: when Instagram fell a day behind YouTube early in the run, the lag was kept by choice rather than fixed with a messy double-post catch-up.

## Outcome

148 Shorts published in total (69 original + the 79-video series), a two-platform daily series running hands-off, and an audience across Japan and Southeast Asia learning one kanji a day.

This channel is customer #1 of [the engine](/work/the-engine) — the same pipeline, productized.
